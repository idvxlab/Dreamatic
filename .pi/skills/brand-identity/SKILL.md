# Brand Identity

Develop new identities, rebrands and extensions from positioning and subject
understanding, then carry the idea into actual applications.

## Position and recognize

Establish audience, promise, personality, cultural context, competitive difference
and practical touchpoints. For extensions preserve established equity; for rebrands
identify authorized changes. Separate confirmed requirements from proposals.

Distinguish the actual subject, research imagery and existing identity assets.
Viewing a logo for research, conditioning an edit with pixels and reproducing an
asset are different operations. Apply usage conditions to the specific asset;
unknown photo permissions do not prohibit depicting the real subject.

Compare faithful depiction, selective stylization and abstraction by recognition
and meaning. Preserve distinguishing silhouette, geometry, rhythm or cultural
details when needed. A five-span landmark should not become an arbitrary bridge
merely to appear original. Do not trace an existing mark or invent landmark facts.

## Develop and select a direction

Explore materially different meaning, mark, composition and verbal strategies,
rather than recolors of one symbol. Extract principles from precedents instead
of borrowing an entire identity. Compare candidates in the same demanding
application, with comparable detail: recognition, differentiated meaning, cultural
fit, expressive range and reproduction tradeoffs.

Develop the chosen device through silhouette, positive/negative space, proportion,
optical balance and typography. Provide related small-use and full-detail versions
when needed. Original metaphors are proposed readings, not documented traditions;
do not claim verified worldwide uniqueness or official authenticity without evidence.

## Build the family

Specify the identity thesis, mark/wordmark relationships, palette roles, type
hierarchy, grid, imagery, motif, materials and voice. Identify actual invariants
and medium-specific adaptations; coherence does not require identical layouts.
Record exact-preservation instructions and evidenced conditions for each existing
asset. A speculative identity must not imply official affiliation.

Design brand names, headlines and application copy alongside the graphics.
Preserve approved strings and include literal wording, placement and typography
in relevant image prompts; unnamed mockups and decorative pseudo-text do not
communicate the identity. Generated lettering remains subject to rendering limits.

## Coverage and judgment

Choose distinct views of marks, lockups, type, campaigns, signage, digital/print
applications, packaging or merchandise as the brief warrants. Significant decisions
need readable outputs; one crowded board is not automatically sufficient. Set
quantity from purpose and user scope, without a fixed quota.

Each image specifies subject features, applicable asset conditions, consistency,
acceptance, composition and size. Judge distinction, recognition, coherence,
legibility, cultural fit and practical reproduction in the actual touchpoints.
